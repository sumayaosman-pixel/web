From a report to a decision

As Head Analyst, our report showed written, educational posts drew almost 4× the engagement of video at similar reach. The committee put written content first, and we reached 15.17% engagement against a 4.5% platform average.

From a hunch to a plan

Travelway expected Ramadan to be a loss. I built a data-backed proposal, split the audience by need, and ran the campaign through to a post-campaign review. It turned into £50,000+ in sales.
