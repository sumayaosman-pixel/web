Group case studies, Queen Mary

Should AI help clinicians reply to patients?

A hospital rolled out an AI tool to draft replies to patient messages. It saved clinicians hours, but 32% of drafts needed significant edits, and one near miss triggered a legal review. Our team looked at why people over-trust automation under time pressure, and what that does to patient trust.

  1. Redesign the workflowCounter time scarcity so review isn't rushed
  2. Stop the “hollow check”Random human audits of AI-approved messages
  3. Be open with patientsDisclose AI use to protect trust
